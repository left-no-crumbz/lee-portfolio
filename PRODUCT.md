## Users

The primary users are recruiters evaluating Lee for roles related to aeronautical engineering. Engineering managers and potential collaborators are secondary audiences.

Recruiters need to determine quickly what Lee built or analyzed, which responsibilities were his, what tools and methods he used, and which outcomes are supported by evidence.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Lee's aeronautical engineering work, practical experience, education, and competition record through concise technical case studies supported by real artifacts. Its primary purpose is to help Lee earn consideration and interviews for aeronautical engineering opportunities.

Success means a recruiter can quickly understand Lee's discipline, individual contribution, technical range, and strongest project evidence, then access his resume or contact him.

## Positioning

This is an evidence-led engineering portfolio rather than a generic personal showcase. It distinguishes Lee's individual contribution from team outcomes and treats CAD work, simulations, fabrication records, test media, documentation, and project limitations as the substance of each case study.

## Operating Context

The work spans academic aircraft and test-equipment projects, engineering competitions, and practical experience. Recurring activities and tools include:

- SolidWorks CAD, assemblies, drawings, and simulation
- XFLR5 stability analysis
- Betaflight configuration
- CFD analysis
- BOM creation, procurement, and spreadsheet management

Visitors will primarily browse the work, inspect supporting images or video, read project-specific responsibilities and constraints, view experience and credentials, and access a resume or contact route.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The portfolio must support a project overview and technical case studies built from the supplied media and written evidence.
- Every team project must distinguish Lee's work from shared outcomes and work completed by teammates or third parties.
- Claims must remain bounded by the available evidence. Unknown measurements, unsupported benchmarks, award attribution, and causal impact must not be invented.
- The RC plane / fixed-wing UAV was not flight-tested before Lee transferred leadership following his move to the United States.
- The quadcopter's approximately 30-minute duration describes multiple stable, low-aggression flights with periods of hovering, the standard battery configuration, and no payload beyond required onboard electronics. The exact trial count is unavailable.
- The wind tunnel was used for visual testing and did not collect numerical experimental data.
- The supersonic wind tunnel model was representational; wind behavior was evaluated through SolidWorks simulation.
- SEAIR work details are confidential. No official reduced-downtime claim exists.
- There is no official evidence that Lee's Klimatech work caused the company's awards or media coverage.
- Project source artifacts are not publicly downloadable. Hiring managers may request access or permission.
- Some RC plane components came from GrabCAD, and some group-created parts may not be shown publicly. Publication must respect group decisions and third-party ownership.
- The exact public display name, contact details, current location wording, availability status, and final resume-download policy remain open decisions.

## Product Principles

1. **Prove the work.** Pair every meaningful claim with a real artifact, method, observation, or clearly stated scope.
2. **Attribute precisely.** Separate Lee's decisions and deliverables from team, third-party, and open-source contributions.
3. **Make evaluation fast.** Let recruiters identify role fit, technical capability, and strongest evidence without decoding vague language.
4. **Protect restricted material.** Respect confidentiality, group publication decisions, third-party ownership, and request-only artifacts.
5. **Be candid about limits.** Present untested states, representational models, unavailable measurements, and uncertain attribution without inflation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must remain keyboard-operable, preserve readable contrast, provide meaningful alternatives for engineering imagery and video, respect reduced-motion preferences, and meet WCAG AA expectations established in `DESIGN.md`.
